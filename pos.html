Questions

Good to know.

Have another question? Call or message us any time.

Do I need internet to use Bardium POS?

The Online edition needs internet so every screen stays in sync. If the counter goes offline, orders still reach the kitchen and billing continues when it reconnects. For places with weak internet, ask us about the Offline edition.

Can I run it on my phone?

Yes. Owners and managers can see running tables, online orders and open or close the store from their phone. Waiters take orders on a phone too.

Will it look like my restaurant?

Yes. Every restaurant gets its own name, colours and look, so your guests see your brand, not ours.

Who sets it up and trains my staff?

We do. We add your menu, tables and floors, and train your counter, waiters and kitchen before you go live.

How do guests pay?

Guests and staff can pay by UPI, quickly and simply.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Each restaurant's data is kept separate and secured, every staff member has their own login, and everything travels over an encrypted connection.
