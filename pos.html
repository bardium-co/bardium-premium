What's inside

Everything your
restaurant needs.

From the first order to the last bill of the day, Bardium POS covers the whole flow, with no extra tools to juggle.

Counter billing

Quick billing on a Windows computer. Add items, apply offers and close the bill in seconds.

Waiter phones

Waiters take orders on an Android phone and send them straight to the kitchen.

Kitchen tickets (KOT)

Every order shows up on the kitchen screen as a live ticket. No shouting, no lost paper.

QR table ordering

Guests scan the table QR, browse the menu and order. They sign in with WhatsApp or Google, see recent orders and pay.

UPI payments

Simple UPI payments for guests and staff, without the fuss of a heavy payment-gateway setup.

Loyalty & offers

A loyalty programme and marketing campaigns, set up and run for you by Bardium.

Your look, your menu

Your menu, your name, your colours. Pick a theme that suits your restaurant or café.

Clear reports

See what sells, what is slow and when you are busiest, all in one place.

Security & privacy

Your business,
secured.

  • Each restaurant's data stays separateYour menu, orders and customers are never mixed with anyone else's.
  • Staff sign in with their own loginEvery person has their own credentials, and sees only what their role needs.
  • Encrypted connectionEverything travels over HTTPS, so data between screens and the server cannot be read.
  • Customer privacy done properlySign-in, consent and data rules are handled carefully and checked with a lawyer.
  • Backups and safe updatesYour data is backed up, and updates go live without breaking your service.
  • Secured by defaultSecurity is part of the product from day one, at no extra cost.
